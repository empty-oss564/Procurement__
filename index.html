⚠️ Current Mandi Ground Problems

Real Distress
  • ❌ Unpredictable Waiting Times: Farmers wait 12–36 hours in tractor lines with no visibility into queue load.
  • ❌ Traffic & Transit Bottlenecks: Unavoidable village road delays cause farmers to miss rigid slots, forfeiting their day's procurement.
  • ❌ Mandi Gate Congestion: Uneven distribution across regional centres causes severe yard bottlenecks.
  • ❌ Opaque Crop Grading & Delayed Payments: Lack of digital receipts and unclear deduction reasons leave farmers vulnerable.

✨ Code Cultivators' Smart Solution

SIH 2026 Innovation
  • ✅ Farmer-Entered Travel Time: Farmers specify actual expected travel time based on ground truth.
  • ✅ Dynamic 25% Threshold Engine: Grace buffer preserves slots for minor delays; automatically reschedules when breached.
  • ✅ Smart Centre Recommendation: Balances distance, queue length, and processing capacity across Mandis.
  • ✅ End-to-End Procurement & DBT: Transparent accepted/rejected quantity breakdown, live MSP formula, and digital e-receipt.
